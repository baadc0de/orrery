# Asset brief: mothership-faction light escort craft ("escort", G2.2b)

Class: single-seat, non-jump-capable escort fighter carried compacted in a ship's inventory and inflated on launch (G4.16).
Role: fleet engagement screen; expendable; nobody owns it.
Silhouette: compact, blunt-nosed, wedge hull about 9 m long, 6 m wingspan, 2.5 m tall on skids. Twin rear thrusters with flip-and-burn plausibility (visible reaction-control thruster clusters at nose and wingtips). Canopy is a narrow armoured slit, not a bubble.
Faction style (mothership, "civilian-industrial navy"): utilitarian, panelled, riveted plating, exposed conduit runs along the spine, painted not polished.
Palette: hull #8a8f94 (warm grey), accent stripe #d9772b (safety orange) along both flanks, dark panels #2b2e33, emissive thruster interiors #7fd4ff, canopy tint #1c3f5a.
Materials: painted steel plating (matte, chipped edges), rubberised dark polymer on RCS clusters and skids, glossy canopy, bare aluminium on fasteners.
Hardpoints: two under-wing pylons (empty), one ventral gun bay.
Decals: hull number stencil "E-07" on left flank; mothership crest placeholder circle on the tail fin.
Moving parts: canopy hinge, thruster nozzle gimbals, landing skids retract.
Scale reference: none in frame.
